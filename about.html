About the Company

Morare Trading and Logistics (Pty) Ltd is a South African–based trading, logistics, and commodities services company focused on the mining, energy, and industrial sectors.

The company provides end-to-end solutions across sourcing, transportation, marketing, and supply of bulk commodities, with a strong emphasis on chrome ore, coal, and related mining products.

Morare Trading and Logistics is positioned as a flexible, compliant, and growth-oriented partner for mines, processors, smelters, and industrial buyers, supporting both domestic and cross-border trade within Southern Africa.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, efficient, and compliant end-to-end trading and logistics solutions for the mining, energy, and industrial sectors across Southern Africa, creating value for our partners through excellence in service delivery.

Our Vision

To be the leading commodities trading and logistics partner in Southern Africa, recognized for our integrity, operational excellence, and commitment to connecting mines, processors, and markets across the region.

Our Values

Purpose-driven operations, unwavering dedication to quality, compliance with all regulatory standards, flexibility in meeting client needs, and a commitment to growth and excellence in everything we do.
