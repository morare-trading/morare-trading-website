What We Offer

Morare Trading and Logistics provides comprehensive end-to-end solutions across the mining, energy, and industrial commodity supply chain.

Crushing & Screening

Our crushing and screening services provide essential processing capabilities for mining operations. We offer professional material reduction and sizing solutions that transform raw mined materials into market-ready products.

  • Primary and secondary crushing operations
  • Multi-stage screening for precise material sizing
  • Quality control and material grading
  • Mobile and fixed plant capabilities
  • High-capacity processing for bulk operations

Chrome Wash Plants

Our chrome wash plant operations utilize advanced processing technology to efficiently recover chrome ore concentrates. We specialize in maximizing chrome recovery rates while maintaining the highest quality standards.

  • Advanced chrome ore beneficiation
  • Gravity separation and spiral concentration
  • High recovery rates for chrome concentrates
  • Environmental compliance and water recycling
  • Tailings management and disposal

Loading & Hauling

Our loading and hauling services ensure the safe and efficient movement of bulk mining commodities. With modern equipment and experienced operators, we deliver reliable transportation solutions.

  • Heavy-duty loading equipment
  • Inter-site and cross-border hauling
  • Fleet management and GPS tracking
  • Safety-compliant operations
  • Bulk material transportation specialists

Stockpile Management

Expert stockpile management services ensuring proper storage, quality preservation, and accurate inventory tracking. We maintain material integrity from point of storage to dispatch.

  • Strategic stockpile placement and management
  • Quality monitoring and sampling
  • Inventory tracking and reporting
  • Contamination prevention measures
  • Material blending and homogenization

Warehousing

Secure and well-managed warehousing facilities for bulk commodity storage. Our warehousing solutions provide safe, organized, and accessible storage with integrated logistics support.

  • Secure storage facilities
  • Climate and condition-appropriate storage
  • Inventory management systems
  • Loading and unloading coordination
  • Distribution logistics integration

Mineral Trading

Expert mineral trading services specializing in chrome ore, coal, and related mining products. We connect mines, processors, smelters, and industrial buyers across domestic and international markets within Southern Africa.

  • Chrome ore sourcing and trading
  • Coal trading and supply
  • Market intelligence and pricing
  • Cross-border trade facilitation
  • Contract negotiation and management
