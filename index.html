Who We Are

Morare Trading and Logistics (Pty) Ltd is a South African–based trading, logistics, and commodities services company focused on the mining, energy, and industrial sectors.

The company provides end-to-end solutions across sourcing, transportation, marketing, and supply of bulk commodities, with a strong emphasis on chrome ore, coal, and related mining products.
