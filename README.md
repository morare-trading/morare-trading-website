# Morare Trading and Logistics Website

Official website for Morare Trading and Logistics (Pty) Ltd - A South African-based trading, logistics, and commodities services company.

## About

Morare Trading and Logistics is focused on the mining, energy, and industrial sectors, providing end-to-end solutions across sourcing, transportation, marketing, and supply of bulk commodities.

## Services

- Crushing & Screening
- Chrome Wash Plants
- Loading & Hauling
- Stockpile Management
- Warehousing
- Mineral Trading

## Technology

- HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
- Responsive design (mobile-friendly)
- SEO optimized with structured data
- Hosted on GitHub Pages
